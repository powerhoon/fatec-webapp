DEPRAG 제품군

Intelligent automation을 전면에 둔 독일의 정밀 조립 기술. screwdriving, feeding, automation, air motors, torque measurement까지 하나의 process chain으로 통합 제공합니다.

DEPRAG — Intelligent Automation

DEPRAG는 screwdriving technology, feeding technology, automation, air motors를 축으로 하는 독일의 정밀 조립 기술 기업입니다. 단품 판매가 아닌 process chain 관점에서 handheld부터 stationary, system solution까지 통합 제안합니다.

파텍시스템은 DEPRAG 제품의 한국 시장 접점으로서 application matching, sizing, engineering service, after-sales support를 제공합니다.

5대 제품군

01

Screwdriving Technology

fastening process를 자동화하거나 안정화하려는 고객에게 가장 먼저 확인할 제품축. handheld, stationary, system solution, torque measuring, software가 하나의 process chain으로 연결됩니다.

Handheld Screwdrivers

  • Electronic handheld screwdrivers
  • Electric handheld screwdrivers
  • Pneumatic handheld screwdrivers
  • Cordless Screwdrivers

Stationary Screwdrivers

  • Electronic stationary screwdrivers
  • Electric stationary screwdrivers
  • Pneumatic stationary screwdrivers

System Solutions

  • Flowhole-forming / ADAPTIVE DFS
  • Screwdriver Function Module (E-SFM)
  • Screwdriving System DEPRAG Plus

Measuring & Software

  • Torque Transducer
  • Digital torque meters
  • DEPRAG Cockpit software
기술영업 포인트: torque range, cycle time, traceability, operator dependence, error proofing 기준으로 접근. feed system과 함께 설계되어야 하는 경우가 많음.
02

Feeding Technology

screwdriving process를 안정적으로 만들기 위한 parts presentation / screw feeding 축. 단품이 아닌 process reliability 요소로 설명해야 합니다.

Feeding Systems

  • Vibratory Bowl Feeder eacy feed
  • Sword Feeder
  • Step Feeder eacy step feed
  • Mini Screw Feeder
  • Screw Presenter / Dispenser
  • Tape-on-Reel Feeder
핵심 질문: screw size, screw type, orientation, supply method, cleanliness requirement
03

Automation

screwdriving, feeding, press-insertion, grinding, manual workstation, assembly line을 통합하는 시스템 제안. machine builder / line integrator 관점에서 접근.

Systems & Lines

  • Assembly systems and lines
  • Automatic screwdriving machines
  • Screwdriving units for robots
  • Manual Workstations

Functional Units

  • Grinding units for robots
  • Press-insertion units
  • Tool changing system
  • Worker guidance system
핵심: integration scope, cycle time, operator flow, maintenance, spare parts, service model
04

Air Motors

pneumatic drive, compact drive, industrial tooling application에서 중요. explosion-safe 환경, compact power source, durability가 필요한 곳에 적합.

Motor Types

  • Vane Motors
  • Turbines
  • Gear motors
  • INDIVIDUAL LINE
핵심: torque, speed, air supply, duty cycle, mounting space, noise. ISO 8573-1 air quality 관리 중요.
05

Torque Measurement

screwdriving 공정의 품질 보증을 위한 torque 측정 기술. traceability, quality control, calibration의 핵심 요소.

Measuring Devices

  • Torque Transducer
  • Digital torque meters
  • Torque sensors
제어 옵션: Torque controlled, Angle controlled, Clamp Force Control (CFC), Friction procedure
