Assembly Technology

Precision fastening, feeding, and automation solutions for demanding manufacturing environments

Fastening Systems

From handheld tools to fully automated screwdriving stations — precision torque control, angle monitoring, and process traceability for Industry 4.0 requirements.

  • Handheld electric screwdrivers with torque/angle control
  • Stationary screwdriving spindles for automated lines
  • Torque measurement and process monitoring
  • Multi-spindle assembly stations

Key Features

  • Torque Range: 0.1 — 500 N·m
  • Process Control: Torque + angle + gradient
  • Traceability: Full data logging and export
  • Integration: PLC, MES, fieldbus ready

Feeding Solutions

  • Vibratory: High-speed bowl feeding
  • Sword: Direct linear feeding
  • Step: Gentle parts handling
  • Custom: Application-specific designs

Material Handling & Feeding

Reliable, consistent parts presentation is critical for automated assembly. Our feeding and material handling solutions ensure steady component flow from bulk supply to workstation.

  • Vibratory bowl feeders for high-volume production
  • Sword and step feeders for controlled parts delivery
  • Integration with pick-and-place and robotic cells

Custom Automation Cells

Quality Assurance

  • Statistical process control (SPC) integration
  • Measurement equipment and calibration services
  • Complete traceability from component to report

Quality Systems

  • Inline QA: 100% process verification
  • Data Export: CSV, SQL, MES integration
  • Certification: ISO-compliant reporting
  • Calibration: Annual service contracts
